## Apify Platform & Compute Usage
The CLI performs scraping by triggering the TikTok scraper actor on the Apify platform ([apify.com](https://apify.com)):
- **Free Tier**: Apify provides \$5 of free compute units each month upon signup, which is sufficient for thousands of profile, video, hashtag, and search scrapes.
- **Paid Plans**: Paid personal and team plans provide higher compute allowances, proxy pools, and increased concurrency.
- **No Hidden Fees**: SpaceCorps charges nothing for the CLI tool or binary distributions.

For official Apify pricing and compute unit allowances, visit [https://apify.com/pricing](https://apify.com/pricing).
